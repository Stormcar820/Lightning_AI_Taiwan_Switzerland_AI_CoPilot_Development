Lightning AI — Legal & Compliance Notice

Project: Lightning AI Taiwan × Switzerland AI Co-Pilot
Repository: Lightning AI Co-Pilot Development
Status: POC / Concept Stage

1. Purpose

Lightning AI is a research and development project exploring an AI assistance layer for existing electric vehicles.

The project may include:

- AI Gateway
- Edge AI
- Computer Vision
- AI Voice
- Navigation AI
- Vehicle Data Integration
- Driver Assistance
- Cybersecurity Architecture
- Software Update / OTA Architecture
- Fleet AI
- AI Safety Layer

The current public repository is a POC / Concept-stage project.

It is not, by itself, a certified automotive product.

7. United States

Where applicable, the project shall evaluate:

- United States federal law;
- applicable state law;
- export controls;
- sanctions;
- intellectual-property law;
- cybersecurity requirements;
- vehicle safety requirements;
- applicable executive orders and federal regulations.

U.S. export-control analysis should determine whether relevant technology, software, hardware, source code, technical information or services are subject to the Export Administration Regulations (EAR) or another export-control regime.

The U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security maintains the EAR at 15 CFR Parts 730–774.

8. Sanctions Screening

Where U.S. persons, U.S.-origin technology, financial institutions, controlled goods or restricted counterparties are involved, applicable sanctions requirements shall be evaluated.

9. Taiwan

Where development, testing, commercialization or data processing occurs in Taiwan, applicable Taiwan laws and regulations shall be reviewed, including requirements relating to:

10. People's Republic of China

Where the project processes vehicle data, personal information or other regulated data within the People's Republic of China, the project shall conduct a separate PRC compliance assessment.

Potentially relevant areas include:

- Cybersecurity Law;
- Data Security Law;
- Personal Information Protection Law;
- automotive data requirements;
- cross-border data transfer;
- export-control requirements;
- algorithm and AI-related requirements.

Automotive data may include vehicle, driver, passenger and external-environment information.

11. Switzerland

Where the project operates in Switzerland or processes Swiss personal data, applicable Swiss data-protection and commercial laws shall be assessed.

Switzerland's revised Federal Act on Data Protection entered into force on 1 September 2023.

12. European Union

Where the project is placed on the EU market or otherwise falls within EU regulatory scope, applicable requirements may include:

- GDPR;
- EU AI Act;
- vehicle type-approval requirements;
- cybersecurity requirements;
- software-update requirements;
- product liability and consumer-protection requirements.

The EU AI Act uses a risk-based regulatory framework. AI systems associated with safety-critical or regulated-product contexts may require additional analysis.

13. Automotive Cybersecurity

If the system becomes part of a regulated vehicle architecture, cybersecurity engineering should consider applicable automotive cybersecurity requirements, including UN Regulation No. 155 where applicable.

14. Software Updates

If the system is integrated into a vehicle and software updates are provided, the applicable software-update regulatory framework should be assessed, including UN Regulation No. 156 where applicable.
