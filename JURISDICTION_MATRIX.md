Compliance Principle

No cross-border transfer should be approved solely because a contract exists.

The project should separately assess:

1. jurisdiction;
2. export control;
3. sanctions;
4. privacy;
5. cybersecurity;
6. IP;
7. vehicle regulation;
8. data localization;
9. contractual restrictions;
10. certification requirements.

High-Risk Combination

The following combination requires enhanced legal review:

«Vehicle + AI + Camera + GPS + Personal Information + Cloud + Cross-Border Transfer + OEM Integration»

This combination should trigger a formal Compliance Gate before production deployment.

U.S. Sanctions

OFAC administers U.S. sanctions programs that may impose asset-blocking and trade restrictions.

Counterparty and transaction screening should therefore be performed where U.S. sanctions jurisdiction may apply.

PRC Automotive Data

PRC automotive-data rules expressly cover automotive data processing and include vehicle-related personal information and important data.

Automotive data compliance must therefore be treated as a dedicated workstream rather than merely a general privacy-policy issue.

Switzerland

Swiss data-protection law was comprehensively revised and the revised Federal Act on Data Protection entered into force on 1 September 2023.

EU / AI

The EU AI Act establishes a risk-based framework for AI systems.

Where AI functionality is incorporated into regulated products or safety-related contexts, classification and applicable conformity obligations must be assessed.

UNECE

For applicable vehicle programs, UN Regulation No. 155 addresses cybersecurity and cybersecurity management systems.

UN Regulation No. 156 addresses software updates and software-update management systems.
